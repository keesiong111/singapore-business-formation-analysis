# 🇸🇬 Singapore Business Formation Analysis (1990–2026)

Analysis of 36 years of Singapore business registration data across 14 industries, 
using open government data from data.gov.sg.

## Key Findings

- **Information & Communications grew +40.8%** post-COVID — the strongest recovery 
  of any sector, signalling sustained demand for tech and data talent
- **Transportation & Storage declined –33.1%** vs pre-COVID baseline — a structural 
  shift driven by platform consolidation (Grab, Gojek), not just COVID
- **Retail Trade surged +54.2% during COVID 2020** — counter-intuitive, but driven 
  by e-commerce registrations as businesses pivoted online
- **Professional Services leads in 2025–2026** at 1,128 new registrations/month, 
  followed by Wholesale Trade (991) and Info-Comms (851)
